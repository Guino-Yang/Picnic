Picnic 2.1 — Bring your MacBook into the sunshine ☀️ 🧺

Click the basket in your menu bar. An outline means standby or off; a filled basket means the display is being boosted.

Auto mode
• By default, 1500 lx for 3 seconds activates 1.5×; 3000 lx for 3 seconds activates 2×.
• Each tier releases at 60% of its activation threshold: by default, ≤1800 lx for 5 seconds drops to 1.5×, and ≤900 lx for 5 seconds turns the boost off.
• Adjust both thresholds with the sliders: 500–5000 lx overall, in 250 lx steps. The second threshold stays at least 250 lx above the first.
• Gain transitions take about 2 seconds. Changing a threshold restarts the timer; your settings are kept for the next launch.

Manual mode always uses 2×. Adjust brightness with your keyboard brightness keys or macOS Control Center.
Off or a normal quit restores the display table owned by Picnic and removes its HDR trigger.

Enable Launch at Login to use the native macOS login item. Normal launches start in Auto mode.
Picnic follows your Mac’s preferred language: English, Japanese, or Simplified Chinese. Relaunch Picnic after changing the language. If none is supported, it uses English.
Only the non-mirrored MacBook built-in display is boosted. There is no separate background daemon or driver.

Compatibility
Built for Apple Silicon and macOS 14+. Observed on a 14-inch M1 Pro MacBook Pro with macOS 27.0.1.
Gain is not a calibrated physical brightness multiplier and does not guarantee 1000 nits. Turn boost off for color-critical work.
The read-only ambient light interface is not a public API; system updates may affect compatibility. Avoid using other display-table dimming tools at the same time.
Picnic adds no pause policy for temperature, low battery, lock, or sleep. macOS display sleep and hardware protections still apply.
Release builds are ad-hoc signed, without Apple Developer ID signing or notarization.
